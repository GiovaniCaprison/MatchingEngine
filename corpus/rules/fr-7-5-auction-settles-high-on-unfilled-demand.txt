# FR-7.5 an auction with unfilled demand settles at the higher price
#
# Both candidates trade thirty and leave a surplus of twenty, and the reference sits exactly between
# them, so volume, surplus and distance all tie. The surplus is unfilled demand, which is buying
# pressure, so the price settles high. Without that rule the bidder who bid the market up would be
# filled below the price they named.

INSTRUMENT tick=5 lot=1 scale=4 min=1 max=1000000 band=500 open=100000 alloc=PRICE_TIME

SESSION    OPENING_AUCTION
STATE      OPENING_AUCTION

NEW        BUY LIMIT GTC 100010 50 p=2
ACCEPTED   #1
RESTED     #1 BUY 100010 50

NEW        SELL LIMIT GTC 99990 30 p=3
ACCEPTED   #2
RESTED     #2 SELL 99990 30
INDICATIVE 100010 30

SESSION    CONTINUOUS
EXECUTED   @1 aggressor=#1 resting=#2 100010 30
STATE      CONTINUOUS
